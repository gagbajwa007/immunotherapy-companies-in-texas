Immunotherapy Companies in Texas

Biotech companies in Texas that develop therapies using the immune system, including engineered T cells, antibodies, innate-immune agonists and regulatory-T-cell approaches. Most are clustered around the Texas Medical Center in Houston, UT Southwestern in Dallas, and Austin.

The Texas immunotherapy ecosystem

MD Anderson Cancer CenterHouston. One of the largest cancer immunotherapy research centers in the world, and the source of several spinouts (e.g. ImmunoGenesis) and licensed CAR-NK programs.
Baylor College of MedicineHouston. Its Center for Cell and Gene Therapy (with Texas Children's and Houston Methodist) is the origin of Marker, March Bio and other T-cell therapy companies.
UT SouthwesternDallas. Research on innate immunity and cGAS-STING that led to companies such as ImmuneSensor.
CPRITThe Cancer Prevention & Research Institute of Texas funds cancer research and gives company-formation grants, which draw many oncology startups to the state.
TMC Innovation / Helix ParkHouston. Texas Medical Center's accelerator and life-science campus for early-stage biotech.
